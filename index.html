Tools

A few small, self-contained things that run entirely in the browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — your work stays on your machine.

Live

Floor Planner

Draw a single room with squared walls and arrange furniture by dragging. Wall lengths are internal, and you can pin the ones you have already settled.
Live

Wheel of Fortune

Pick a category or write your own entries, then click the wheel to spin. You can cap how many spins you are allowed before it locks.
